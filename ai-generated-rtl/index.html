The failure mode

Where GenAI RTL breaks: control logic

LLMs handle the easy, repetitive parts of a chip well. Where they slip is the coordination logic — and those bugs are exactly the kind no amount of testing can rule out.

Technical detail

LLMs are respectable at datapaths — pipelines, arithmetic, muxing. Where generated designs come apart is control: arbitration, handshakes, mutual exclusion, fairness, backpressure, interlocks. Control logic is order-sensitive and adversarial — the environment gets a move every cycle, and one missed corner is a hang or a race.

That kind of bug passes lint. It passes a handful of directed tests. And then it ships — because no finite sample of traces can witness “this deadlock is unreachable” or “every requester is eventually served.” External studies put roughly 60% of LLM-generated hardware designs as carrying weaknesses in MITRE’s hardware-CWE catalog; the control classes are exactly where sampling-based checks go blind.

The division of labor

Let the AI do what it’s good at, and let Mununu own the part it isn’t: the AI writes the bulk of the design, Mununu builds the control core and checks the whole thing.

the LLM

Writes the datapath

What it’s good at: structure, arithmetic, plumbing, the bulk of the RTL — and the spec it means to meet.

mununu · synthesize

Builds the control kernel

Correct-by-construction: a winning strategy that structurally cannot violate mutex or fairness. Synthesized — not generated, then checked.

mununu · verify

Verifies the whole system

The integrated design — datapath plus kernel — is verified end to end, discharging the very assumptions the kernel was synthesized under.

The checker confirms; it does not rescue. Synthesize the control kernel instead of hallucinating it, and a whole class of LLM failure modes never enters the design at all.

Straight answers

FAQ

Can I embed it in my product?

Yes. Mununu is priced per run, not per seat, and is self-hostable: a Rust binary with a CLI and a served HTTP API you can call from your SaaS or agent loop. Your customers don’t need their own licenses for your verify step to run.

Does it replace JasperGold or VC Formal?

No — it’s a complement, and it can wrap industrial-scale engines rather than beat them. Those tools own scale; Mununu occupies a different region of the map: branching-time properties like AG EF good, reactive controller synthesis, and embeddability behind a per-run API.

How does it handle LLM hallucinations in the control logic?

By keeping them out of the control path. Mununu synthesizes the control kernel correct-by-construction — a winning strategy that structurally cannot violate its specification — rather than checking hallucinated control logic after the fact. The checker confirms; it doesn’t rescue.

What can’t it do yet?

Full-SoC sign-off. Honest scale is block and FSM level, with an exact cone of roughly 40 bits. Above that, it returns ⊥ (undecided) rather than guessing — and it’s Alpha software. We’d rather abstain than bluff.

Is the LLM part included?

No — you bring the model. Mununu is the synthesis-and-verification oracle your model calls: it takes RTL and a spec, and returns verdicts, synthesized control kernels, and structured JSON counterexamples your model can consume.

Is it fast at synthesis?

Dedicated synthesizers synthesize faster. Mununu’s claim is the integration: synthesis and whole-system verification behind one embeddable API, with the kernel’s assumptions discharged against the actual assembled design.
